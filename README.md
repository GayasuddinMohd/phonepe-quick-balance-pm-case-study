# 🚀 Product Case Study: Optimising PhonePe's Quick-Balance Journey

An end-to-end Product Management case study focusing on reducing friction in the UPI balance-checking and payment flow.

---

## 1. Project Overview & Objective
* **Feature Name:** Quick Balance Sneak-Peek (Biometric Fast Balance View)
* **Objective:** Reduce the time-to-value for balance checking by 50% while maintaining strict UPI security standards.

## 2. Problem Statement (The "Why")
* **Current Journey:** Opening the app ➔ Clicking "Check Bank Balance" ➔ Selecting Bank ➔ Entering 4/6-digit UPI PIN ➔ Waiting for bank server response.
* **The Friction:** Forcing a full UPI PIN verification just to view balance creates high friction and unnecessary server load, especially during peak commute hours or slow internet conditions.

## 3. User Persona
* **Name:** Rahul, 24 (Daily Commuter & Quick Shopper)
* **Behavior:** Uses UPI apps 4–5 times a day at local vendors, grocery shops, and transit points.
* **Pain Point:** Experiences payment anxiety regarding sufficient account balance right before scanning a merchant QR code in crowded places.

## 4. Proposed Solution: "Quick Balance Sneak-Peek"
Instead of a mandatory UPI PIN login, introduce a biometric-powered quick view:
1. **Home Screen Widget:** A subtle "Eye" icon next to the primary bank account.
2. **One-Touch Auth:** Uses device-native hardware lock (Fingerprint / Face ID) instantly.
3. **Instant Fetch:** Displays cached/fast-read balance inline without hitting the core NPCI transactional gateway.

## 6. Success Metrics (KPIs)
* **Adoption Rate:** Percentage of Daily Active Users (DAU) utilizing the Quick Balance feature.
* **Drop-off Reduction:** Decrease in payment failures caused by "Insufficient Funds".
* **Task Completion Time:** Reducing balance check time from ~8 seconds to under 3 seconds.
